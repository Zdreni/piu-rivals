piu-rivals privacy policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026

piu-rivals is a small, non-commercial site where Pump It Up players compare their scores with their rivals'. It is run by one person as a hobby project and is not affiliated with Andamiro or with PIU Scores (piuscores.arroweclip.se). This page explains what data piu-rivals keeps and why.

Signing in with Google

You sign in with your Google account. Google then tells piu-rivals your email address and your name. piu-rivals asks for nothing else: no contacts, no files, no other Google data.

Your email is used only to recognize you when you sign in. Only accounts that the site's administrator has added can use piu-rivals; signing in with any other account is refused.

What is stored about your account

  • Your email address and display name
  • The PIU Scores player linked to your account, if any
  • Your list of rivals and your site settings
  • When your account was created and when you last signed in
  • Your sign-in sessions. The site keeps a random sign-in token in your browser's local storage; the server stores only a one-way hash of it. Sessions expire after 30 days or when you sign out.

Player scores

piu-rivals shows players' names, PUMBILITY and play results (scores, grades and judgments). This data is collected from the public pages of PIU Scores, where anyone can see it. It is not linked to your Google account, except for the one player an administrator links to your account.

Where the data is kept and who sees it

  • All data is stored in a database on the piu-rivals server.
  • It is never sold, used for advertising, or shared with anyone else.
  • piu-rivals uses no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking cookies.
  • Your browser keeps your sign-in token and a copy of the score data you view, so the site loads quickly. Signing out removes the token.
  • Google processes your sign-in under Google's privacy policy.

Deleting your data

Your account stays until you ask for it to be removed. To have your account deleted, or a player's scores removed from piu-rivals, send an email to the address below. Deleting an account removes your email, name, rivals, settings and sessions.
